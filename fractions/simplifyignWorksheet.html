✏️ The Cancelling Trick

Use a common factor to divide both parts of the fraction.

8 12 →
÷ 4 ÷ 4
→ 2 3
⭐ GOLDEN RULE ⭐
Whatever you divide the TOP by,
you MUST divide the BOTTOM by.
8 ÷ 4 = 2    and    12 ÷ 4 = 3

💡 Why Can We Cancel?

Look at the hidden multiplication inside the fraction.

2 × 4 3 × 4 = 2 3
The same factor 4 is on TOP and BOTTOM.

Dividing both by 4 removes the matching factor.
(2 × 4) ÷ 4 = 2
(3 × 4) ÷ 4 = 3
