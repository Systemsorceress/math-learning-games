Lesson 1

Express a Number as a Fraction of Another Number

Today we will learn how to express one number as a fraction of another number.

A as a fraction of B
↓
A ÷ B = A/B

First number → TOP

Second number → BOTTOM

Key Idea

What Does It Mean?

Suppose there are:

1
4 red balls
2
10 balls altogether
3
Find the fraction that are red
4 as a fraction of 10
↓
4/10
↓
2/5
Answer = 2/5
Golden Rule ⭐

The Golden Rule

“A as a fraction of B”

= A/B

Example

Express 6 as a fraction of 15.

6/15 → 2/5

Answer: 2/5

Important

Don't Reverse the Numbers! ⚠️

Express 3 as a fraction of 12.

3/12 = 1/4
❌ 12/3 is wrong.

The number being described goes on TOP.
First number → TOP
Second number → BOTTOM

Decimals

What If the Numbers Are Decimals?

Express 0.4 as a fraction of 2.

0.4/2

Convert 0.4 into a fraction:

0.4 = 4/10

So:

0.4/2 = 4/20 = 1/5
Answer = 1/5
Decimals

Another Decimal Example

Express 1.5 as a fraction of 6.

1.5/6

Multiply both numbers by 10:

15/60

Simplify:

1/4
⭐ If you multiply the numerator by 10, you must also multiply the denominator by 10.
Common Mistakes

Watch Out! ⚠️

1. Reversing the numbers

Express 8 as a fraction of 20.

❌ 20/8

✅ 8/20 = 2/5

2. Forgetting to simplify

❌ 6/18

✅ 1/3

3. Dividing only one number

Always divide the numerator AND denominator by the same number.

Remember

The 3-Step Method

1

Write

Put the first number on top.

A/B
2

Simplify

Divide the numerator and denominator by a common factor.

3

Check

Make sure the fraction is in its simplest form.
