🟡 Part 3 & 4

Different Denominators

What should you do when denominators are different?

Step 1

Find a common denominator.

Step 2

Convert into equivalent fractions.

Step 3

Compare the numerators!

Example:

Order 12, 14, 34. Common denominator = 4.

Since 12 = 24, we compare 24, 14, 34 14 < 12 < 34
