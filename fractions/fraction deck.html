05 / Learn

The Secret Rule

To make an equivalent fraction, multiply or divide the numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number.

Same operation, top and bottom.

Both numbers must change in the same way.

Multiply

23 × 22
↓
46

Two thirds is four sixths.

Divide

46 ÷ 22
↓
23

Only divide when both numbers are divisible by the same number.

Which action makes an equivalent fraction?
Choose an answer.
Teacher support

Ask: Why must the same operation happen to both numbers?

Expected answer: Otherwise the value of the fraction changes.

09 / Think critically

Spot the Mistake

A student tries to make an equivalent fraction. Can you find the error?

2/3 = 4/5

“I multiplied the numerator by 2 and added 2 to the denominator.”

What mistake did the student make?
Choose the mistake.
2/3 × 2/2 = 4/6

Both numbers must be multiplied by the same number. Adding 2 to the denominator is not the same operation.

Teacher support

Ask: Is 3 × 2 equal to 5? What operation made 3 become 5?

Expected answer: The student added 2 to the denominator instead of multiplying it.
