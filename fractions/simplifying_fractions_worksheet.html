1. Warm-Up: What is a Factor?

A factor divides a number exactly, with no remainder.

Example: Factors of 12 are: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
Question: Which number is a factor of 18?

2. What Does “Simplify” Mean?

To simplify a fraction, we make an equivalent fraction with smaller numbers.

68 = 34
Remember: Whatever you divide the numerator by, divide the denominator by the same number.

Example: Simplify 8/12

Find a common factor: 4
Divide both: 8 ÷ 412 ÷ 4 = 23
Do not do this: 8 ÷ 4 and 12 ÷ 2. The same divisor must be used for both.
