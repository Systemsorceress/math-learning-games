Interactive Practice — Build the Division Ladder

Build a separate ladder for each answer. In every row, type the prime you are dividing by on the left, then the two results on the line below (a number that will not divide is carried straight down). Press Check Step — each step is checked.

A · HCF Ladder

Divide only by primes that go into both numbers. Stop when the two numbers share no common factor. HCF = the divisors on the left multiplied together.

B · LCM Ladder

Divide by any prime that goes into at least one number (common primes first). Keep going until both numbers are 1. LCM = every divisor on the left multiplied together.
