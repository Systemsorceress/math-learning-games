03 / HCF division ladder

Let's solve the fruit-box problem

Numbers: 12 and 18. Divide both numbers by the same prime factor.

HCF rule: Only divide by a prime factor that divides BOTH numbers.

Why do we stop?

When the current numbers have no common prime factor, the common division has finished.

The divisors on the left are the common prime factors.

05 / LCM

Two buses, one stop

Bus A arrives every 4 minutes. Bus B arrives every 6 minutes. Both arrive at 9:00 AM.

What are we finding?

Bus A: Every 4 minutes

Bus B: Every 6 minutes

LCM = 12
Both buses arrive together again after 12 minutes, at 9:12 AM.
06 / LCM division ladder

Find the first shared multiple

Numbers: 4 and 6. This time, a prime may divide AT LEAST ONE of the numbers.

LCM rule: Divide by a prime if it divides at least one of the numbers. If it does not divide a number, carry that number down unchanged.

Remember

For LCM, every divisor is important because we are collecting all prime factors needed to reach 1 and 1.
